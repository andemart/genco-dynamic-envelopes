# Data provenance and licences

The Apache-2.0 licence in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) covers the project code and documentation. It does not cover third-party data in this repository, which keep their own terms.

| Path | What it is | Source and terms |
|---|---|---|
| `data/feeder_topology/ENWL1/` | OpenDSS files of ENWL LV network 1 as a three-phase four-wire model: line codes with 4×4 phase-and-neutral matrices, loads, coordinates, master file | Network topology and loads: Electricity North West (ENWL), Low Voltage Network Solutions project: https://www.enwl.co.uk/future-energy/innovation/smaller-projects/low-carbon-networks-fund/low-voltage-network-solutions/ (terms as published by ENWL). The 4×4 line impedances are identical to CSIRO network_1 (row below), which derives them from Urquhart & Thomson, *Cable impedance data*, Loughborough University, https://hdl.handle.net/2134/15544 , **CC BY-NC 3.0 (non-commercial)**. Cable ratings (`NormAmps`): source not recovered (the file first appears in commit 1222968); the Waveform ratings equal ENWL CP203 Table 6, the BS 6480 Cu 16 rating (101 A) differs from CP203 Table 12 (89 A); see `docs/fourwire_scale_protocol.md` §2.4. Modifications in this repository: the neutral is earthed only at the transformer (`Reactor.grndTR`; the source-side earth is commented out), and load shapes are replaced by the household profiles below at run time. |
| `data/feeder_topology/ENWL_CSIRO/` | The four-wire variants of all 25 ENWL LV networks (128 feeders) as OpenDSS files, unchanged, with the dataset's own `README.md` and `LICENSE.txt` | CSIRO, *Four-wire low voltage power network dataset* (Heidarihaei, Geth, Claeys, Urquhart), https://doi.org/10.25919/jaae-vc35 , **CC BY 4.0**, derived from the ENWL Low Voltage Network Solutions models. **Mixed terms:** its 4×4 line impedances derive from Urquhart & Thomson, *Cable impedance data*, https://hdl.handle.net/2134/15544 , **CC BY-NC 3.0 (non-commercial)**. No cable ratings are included; the ratings used are documented in `docs/fourwire_scale_protocol.md` §2.4 (ENWL CP203, cited by URL, not redistributed). |
| `data/load_profiles/*.xlsx` | Legend workbook of the Fluvius digital-meter dataset | Part of the publicly available Fluvius open dataset (row below). Terms: Fluvius open-data licence, https://opendata.fluvius.be/p/licentieopendatafluvius |
| Fluvius meter profiles (not in the repository, publicly available online) | 2,400 anonymised residential digital meters, quarter-hourly, 2024, eight technology groups | Fluvius Open Data: https://opendata.fluvius.be/explore/assets/1_50-verbruiksprofielen-dm-elek-kwartierwaarden-voor-een-volledig-jaar/ . Download so the CSVs sit at `data/load_profiles/<folder>/P6269_Open_Data_<group>.csv`. |
| `data_out/` (not in the repository) | Derived run files, four-wire labels, features, LV checkpoints | Derived from the Fluvius meters. Not yet redistributed while the licence terms for meter-level derivatives are checked; SHA-256 hashes in `results/run_manifest_*.json`. |
| Official GENCO checkpoint (not in the repository) | `gridfm/genco-opf-datakit`, case118 | https://huggingface.co/gridfm/genco-opf-datakit, under its own licence. |
