It is a new experiment family, E4: on all 24 eligible networks, envelopes designed with a balanced model against four-wire-verified envelopes, checked on fresh draws. It asks whether the ENWL1 result of the paper draft ("balanced-model envelopes fail the four-wire check") holds on the other networks. No learned model and no label is involved. Nothing of it has run on a held-out day.

The author fixed three design choices before any code or dry run existed, knowing the ENWL1 result and the committed E2 envelope records: (1) the balanced model is the same network and sweep with every customer's power spread equally over the three phases; (2) the criterion is a per-network count (a network is "balanced unsafe" if fewer than half of its positive balanced envelopes reach 0.95 four-wire feasibility on 200 fresh draws; replicates if at least 12 of the 23 fresh networks); (3) the check uses the validated sweep, plus OpenDSS on the fresh states of three days per network.

The dry-run figures in the amendment's disclosure (networks 25, 1, 17 on validation days, on the laptop) are not in the repository; treat them as claims.
