{\color{navy}\fontsize{17.5}{19}\selectfont\bfseries Four-wire-verified dynamic operating envelopes with GENCO}\par
\vspace{2pt}
{\color{blue}\normalsize Where a grid foundation model helps on four-wire LV networks, where it fails, and how we adapted it}\par
\vspace{3pt}

\colorbox{pale}{\parbox{\dimexpr\linewidth-2\fboxsep\relax}{
\textbf{In one sentence.} On a four-wire LV benchmark feeder with real Flemish smart-meter households, dynamic envelopes free several MWh of capacity per household per year where a constant optional add-on frees none. We made GENCO work on LV, put it inside an exact four-wire check, extended a GENCO-style model to explicit phase and neutral states, and benchmarked every GENCO role against strong classical methods. We report honestly where it adds value.
}}

\vspace{3pt}
\begin{multicols}{2}

\sect{The application}

Operators must host EVs, heat pumps and rooftop PV without reinforcing the grid. A \textbf{dynamic operating envelope} (DOE) gives each household a time-varying import/export limit.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Feeder:} ENWL1, 800\,kVA, 200 single-phase customers, populated with Fluvius 2024 profiles.
  \item \textbf{Scenarios:} 200 per interval, resampled real meters.
  \item \textbf{Acceptance:} every envelope from the exact search or a gated variant must pass an \textbf{exact four-wire OpenDSS check} of a frozen rule ($\geq$98\% of scenarios feasible).
\end{itemize}

\begin{center}
\textbf{Scenarios $\rightarrow$ proposal (exact / GENCO-screened / learned)}\\
\textbf{$\rightarrow$ exact four-wire check $\rightarrow$ envelope}
\end{center}

\sect{Impact: capacity a constant add-on cannot free}

\begin{center}
\metric{4.81 MWh}{export envelope-hours / household / yr}\hfill
\metric{2.50 MWh}{import envelope-hours / household / yr}\\[6pt]
\metric{0}{constant optional add-on}\hfill
\metric{2--3$\times$}{balanced-model envelopes: four-wire unsafe}
\end{center}

\begin{itemize}
  \item 36 test-split days; intervals export 4.35--5.30 and import 2.11--2.87\,MWh.
  \item Robust across 5 household placements. The headline placement is the most conservative.
  \item Idealised three-phase HP/EV connection lifts capacity 2--2.5$\times$.
\end{itemize}

\sect{GENCO development: making it work on LV}

Direct transfer to a 398-bus LV feeder failed. Stock GENCO collapsed and lost to predicting the training mean. We diagnosed and fixed three causes:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Impedance blindness:} the edge encoder's final LayerNorm cancels $|Y|$. Fixed with asinh admittance features.
  \item \textbf{Physics-loss collapse} at LV scales. Fixed by a linear power-flow prior with a zero-initialised correction head.
  \item \textbf{Ill-conditioned flows} from voltage differences. Fixed with Kirchhoff-current-law flows.
\end{itemize}
Result: voltage MAE \textbf{1.0e-3\,pu}. We also found a graphkit best-model export bug and a datakit 1.5\,pu slack bug.

\columnbreak

\sect{Four-wire GENCO-style extension (exploratory)}

A GNN on the 397-bus \textbf{four-wire} graph (phases a, b, c and neutral, full $4{\times}4$ impedances) corrects a one-step four-wire sweep prior. On 2,968 test-split states:

\begin{center}
\metric{$-$91\%}{phase-to-neutral voltage error}\hfill
\metric{35.8\%$\rightarrow$5.8\%}{states sent to OpenDSS}
\end{center}

There were zero observed false-safe decisions under the exact gate. Balanced models send 63\% to OpenDSS.

\sect{Benchmarks: every GENCO role vs strong baselines}

{\small\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash\bfseries\color{navy}}p{0.27\linewidth} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X}
Screening (C/C+) & GENCO + exact gate matches the exact search on 100\% of intervals, 6\% fewer exact calls. Classical screens do as well.\\
Learned proposals & 79\% of exact capacity with \textbf{6.7\% of the exact calls}, but phase features alone do the same. GENCO arm: $-$0.06\,pp.\\
GENCO embeddings & Frozen balanced-GENCO embeddings made the four-wire model \textbf{worse} (+6.0\,pp fallbacks).\\
Classical physics & An iterated four-wire sweep is 80$\times$ more accurate than any learned model. It remains the accuracy leader.\\
\end{tabularx}}

\sect{What we do not claim}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Unconditional safety. Envelopes pass a finite design-sample rule; only 47\% pass a 95\% lower-bound test on fresh draws.
  \item A GENCO advantage over specialised classical solvers, or a wall-clock speed-up. We report exact-call reductions.
  \item Generalisation beyond one benchmark feeder.
\end{itemize}

\sect{Open science and rigour}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Pre-registered core evaluation. Every other study was frozen before its results were seen, with all deviations disclosed as amendments.
  \item Incidents found and disclosed: wrong voltage base, OpenDSS engine poisoning, stale cluster caches.
  \item 84 automated tests. \texttt{reproduce\_check.py} regenerates every headline result byte for byte. Run manifests hash every artifact.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Potential:} one model across LV, MV and HV for TSO--DSO coordination; LV envelopes sum to the flexibility a DSO offers the TSO.
